Sources & notes

Every trophy guide in this app is written in our own words for this guide. The facts come from the sources below; each trophy names its sources beside its guide, so you can check any claim. Trophy names and descriptions are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them.

    How to read this guide

    • Unconfirmed means the launch-week guides had not yet confirmed a method. We say so plainly instead of inventing one — check the linked sources for updates.
    • Hidden trophies (28 of 55) stay hidden on PlayStation until you earn them. The spoiler setting hides story-trophy names and descriptions until you tick them earned.
    • Cheats disable trophies and saving for that session — keep them off on a trophy run.
    • There are no online trophies and no difficulty-related trophies. One playthrough is enough, and free roam continues after the final mission.
